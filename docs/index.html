Learn the mechanics

Fat tails

Option models assume a bell curve, so they underprice rare crashes. Real markets crash far more often than "normal" math predicts — that gap is the opportunity.

Theta (time decay)

An out-of-the-money option loses value every day nothing happens. Monthly options bleed slower than weeklies — that's why this scan targets 30–60 days to expiry.

Vega (volatility)

You can profit from a fear spike without any crash: when VIX jumps, your option's implied volatility reprices it up. The exit rule sells into that panic.

Skew

Index puts further from the money carry higher implied vol because institutions constantly buy crash insurance. So far-OTM puts are rarely "dirt cheap" — the scanner measures skew instead of assuming it away.
