Learn the mechanics

Fat tails

Option models assume a bell curve, so they underprice rare crashes. That gap is the tail-risk opportunity.

Theta & Vega

Options bleed value daily (theta) but reprice up when fear spikes (vega) — you can profit from a VIX jump without a crash.

Skew & VRP

Index puts carry higher IV (institutions buy crash insurance). Volatility risk premium = IV − realized; low/negative = options genuinely cheap.

Momentum, trend & low-vol

Winners persist; holding only above the 200-SMA (else cash) and weighting calmer ETFs more cuts drawdowns — the loss-averse edge.
